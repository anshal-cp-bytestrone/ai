TEACHABLE MACHINE / IMAGE MODEL

What does your model see?

Connect your model, choose an image, and see its predictions with one click.

1. Connect your model

In Teachable Machine, select Export Model > TensorFlow.js > Upload my model, then paste the shareable link here. You can add it later.

2. Choose your image

Image source

Choose a browser-supported image, up to 10 MB.

Allow camera access when prompted. Use localhost or HTTPS if your browser blocks camera access.

Your image preview will appear here

The model sees the center square of your image. Webcam predictions use the mirrored view shown here.

Add a model URL and an image to get started.

3. Prediction results

Ready when you are

Click Classify image to see each class and its confidence score. These are model estimates, not guarantees.

Images stay in your browser. Internet access is needed to download the libraries and your model; no image is uploaded for prediction.
